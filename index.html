Privacy Policy – ShapeFu

Effective Date: 12 May 2026

1. Overview

ShapeFu is a mobile game that does not require user accounts and does not use a backend server. All gameplay data is stored locally on the user's device.

2. Data Storage

All game data, including but not limited to:

  • Player profiles
  • Scores and statistics
  • Settings and preferences
  • Game progress

is stored locally on the user's device only.
No data is transmitted to or stored on external servers.

3. User Accounts

ShapeFu does not require account creation or login.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ShapeFu may offer in-app purchases and subscriptions through the Google Play billing system.

These purchases are:

  • Processed by Google Play services
  • Linked to the user's Google account

Important:

  • If the app is deleted or device cache is cleared, local game data will be lost
  • However, purchased items and subscriptions can be restored by reinstalling the app and signing in with the same Google account used for purchase

5. Data Loss

Because all gameplay data is stored locally:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Clearing app data or cache
  • Resetting the device

will permanently delete local progress and settings.
This does not affect purchased entitlements that are tied to the user's Google Play account.

6. Third-Party Services

ShapeFu may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Billing (for purchases)
  • Advertising services (if enabled in future updates)

These services may collect limited technical data as required for their functionality.

7. Data Collection

ShapeFu does not collect, store, or process personal data on external servers.

8. Security

Since all data remains on the device, security is dependent on the user's device security settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in future versions of ShapeFu. Any changes will be reflected within the app or store listing.
