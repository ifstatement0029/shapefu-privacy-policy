Privacy Policy – ShapeFu

Version: 1

Effective Date: 26 May 2026

1. Overview

ShapeFu is a mobile game available on supported mobile platforms, including Android and iOS devices.

ShapeFu can be played offline for core gameplay functionality. The game does not require user accounts and does not use developer-owned backend servers.

2. Data Storage

Gameplay data is stored locally on the user's device only, including:

  • Player profiles
  • Scores and statistics
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Progress and unlocks
  • Gameplay-related configuration data

Gameplay data is not transmitted to external servers operated by the developer.

3. User Accounts

ShapeFu does not require account creation or login to play.

4. Internet Connectivity

Some features of ShapeFu require an active internet connection, including:

  • In-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Store purchase verification and restoration
  • Accessing external web links such as legal pages

If the device is offline, these features may be unavailable until an internet connection is restored.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ShapeFu may offer optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through platform app stores, including:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Apple App Store In-App Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions:

  • Are processed securely by the respective platform provider
  • Are linked to the user’s platform account
  • May be restorable using the same store account previously used for purchase

The developer does not store payment card details or financial information.

6. Advertising

ShapeFu may display advertisements, including interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements, provided by third-party advertising partners.

These services may collect limited technical and device-related information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App interaction and usage data
  • General diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for:

  • Serving advertisements
  • Measuring advertisement performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Improving service reliability

Data handled by third-party advertising providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Services

ShapeFu may use third-party services including, but not limited to:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Apple App Store In-App Purchases
  • Google Mobile Ads
  • Platform-specific analytics or store services required for app functionality

These services may process technical information required for functionality, purchases, advertising, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and legal compliance.

8. Data Collection

ShapeFu does not collect, store, or process personal information on developer-owned servers.

However, platform providers and third-party services used by the game may process limited technical information necessary for purchases, advertising, diagnostics, and service operation.

9. Data Loss

Since gameplay data is stored locally on the device:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Clearing app data or cache
  • Resetting the device
  • Device failure or corruption

may permanently delete local game progress, settings, and profiles.

Purchases and subscriptions tied to the user’s store account may be restorable through the applicable app store.

10. Security

Gameplay data remains on the user’s device. Device security and access protection are the responsibility of the user.

11. Children's Privacy

ShapeFu does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party advertising, billing, and platform services may process limited technical information as required for service delivery and platform functionality.

12. External Links

ShapeFu may contain links to external websites, including:

  • Privacy Policy pages
  • Terms and Conditions pages
  • Feedback or support contact links

The developer is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Updated versions may be made available through:

  • The app
  • The app store listing
  • The official policy webpage

Continued use of ShapeFu after updates may constitute acceptance of the revised policy.
