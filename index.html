Privacy Policy – ShapeFu

Effective Date: 26 May 2026

1. Overview

ShapeFu is a mobile game that can be played offline for core gameplay. The game does not require user accounts and does not use a backend server.

2. Data Storage

All gameplay data is stored locally on the user's device only, including:

  • Player profiles
  • Scores and statistics
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Progress and unlocks

No gameplay data is transmitted to external servers by the developer.

3. User Accounts

ShapeFu does not require account creation or login.

4. Internet Connectivity

Some features of ShapeFu require an active internet connection, including:

  • In-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements

If the device is offline, these features will not be available until an internet connection is restored.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ShapeFu offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

  • Payments are processed securely by Google Play
  • Purchases are linked to your Google account
  • The developer does not store payment information

6. Advertising

ShapeFu may display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded ads, provided by third-party ad networks.

These third-party services may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • General usage and interaction data

This data is used to serve and measure ads and is handled according to the respective third-party privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Services

ShapeFu may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing (for purchases)
  • Google Mobile Ads (for advertisements)

These services may collect data as required for functionality and compliance.

8. Data Collection

ShapeFu does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to developer-owned servers.

9. Data Loss

Since all data is stored locally:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Clearing app data or cache
  • Resetting the device

will permanently delete all local game progress and settings. Purchased items may be restored via Google Play using the same account.

10. Security

All data remains on the user’s device. Security depends on the device’s own security settings.

11. Children's Privacy

ShapeFu does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Third-party ad and billing providers may process limited technical data as required for service delivery.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be reflected within the app or store listing.
